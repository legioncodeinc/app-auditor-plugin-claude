## For AI agents

- Use the **Pexels** or **Pixabay** API. Both are free with a key, return 4K files, and need no credit on the clip itself (credit the source where you show search results).
- Do not script downloads from Mixkit.
- Show the user 2 or 3 candidates and download only the one they pick.

## Every time you download

Write `intro/SOURCE.md` with:

- the clip page URL and the creator,
- the license name and its URL,
- the date you downloaded it, and
- a screenshot of the license section if the page has one.

Skip clips with logos, readable brand names, famous landmarks or products, or a recognizable person who would look like they endorse the client. None of these sites provide model or property releases. Deliver only the finished edit to the client, never the raw stock files.
