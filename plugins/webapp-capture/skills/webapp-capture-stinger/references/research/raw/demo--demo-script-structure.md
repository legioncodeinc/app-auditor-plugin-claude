## Recommended structure

The article outlines a scripting process organized around a "Tell-Show-Tell" style method, broken into five timed sections:

1. **Hook (10-15 seconds)**: "Open with the buyer's specific pain point or business problem. Do not start with the product name."
2. **Aha! Moment (15-20 seconds)**: Present the single most valuable capability the product delivers.
3. **Solution Walkthrough (45-60 seconds)**: Demonstrate three core features maximum, with each feature addressing a specific problem and delivering a measurable outcome.
4. **Social Proof (10 seconds)**: Include one concrete customer result with a timeframe.
5. **Call to Action (10 seconds)**: End with one specific, low-friction next step.

## Timing guidelines

- **Total video length**: 60-90 seconds for optimal engagement.
- **Word count**: approximately 150 words for a 60-second video; 225 words for a 90-second video.
- **Speaking pace assumed**: 130-150 words per minute.

## Key principle (verbatim-paraphrase)

"Start with the outcome, not the product. Open with the buyer's specific pain. The product earns its place once the problem is real to them."
