## General best-practice guidance commonly attributed to Microsoft's documentation conventions (cross-referenced during research, not verbatim from the fetched page)

- Lead every instruction with an action verb for maximum clarity.
- Crop to the relevant area to reduce noise and keep focus tight, rather than showing full-screen captures.
- Check every screenshot for email addresses, passwords, customer names, and internal URLs before publishing.
- Keep a consistent visual style (arrow styles, colors, image sizing) across a doc set.
- Re-capture screenshots when the underlying UI changes; outdated screenshots cause more confusion than no screenshots.
